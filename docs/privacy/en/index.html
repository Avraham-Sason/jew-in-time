Privacy Policy

Effective 1 September 2026 · Developer: Avraham Sason · App: Jewish Time / יהודי בזמן (com.jewintime.app)

The app runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no server, and no collection of personal data. Nothing you enter and nothing the app computes is sent to us or to any third party.

What the app stores

Every item below is kept only in the app's local storage on your device:

  • Location — if you grant location permission, the coordinates are used to calculate halachic times. You can instead pick a city manually and never grant the permission.
  • Notification permission status — so the app knows whether reminders can be scheduled.
  • Preferences — nusach, language, theme, Israel/Diaspora, halachic opinions, which mitzvot are enabled, and their reminder settings.
  • Completion history — which mitzvot you marked done or skipped, and when.
  • Custom mitzvot you create.
  • Name and phone number — fully optional fields. You may skip them; they are not validated, are displayed nowhere, and are never transmitted.

Where data is stored

Data lives in the app's private storage on your device, protected by the operating system's app sandbox. We keep no cloud backup and perform no cross-device sync.

What does leave the device

The app's own code makes no network requests. The only outbound traffic comes from Expo's update mechanism (EAS Update): the app asks Expo's servers whether a newer JavaScript bundle exists. That check transmits technical values only — app version, platform, and update identifier. It carries no personal data, no location, and no mitzvah history.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, no behavioural tracking, no crash reporting.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No sign-up, no login, no cloud profile.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Location (precise/approximate) — to compute sunrise, sunset, and the halachic times derived from them. Optional; choosing a city by hand is a full substitute.
  • Notifications — to show local reminders at the times you configured.
  • Exact alarms — halachic times are exact moments; without this permission Android may delay a reminder by tens of minutes.
  • Run at boot — so scheduled reminders survive a device restart.
  • Background refresh — to rebuild the day's reminders once every 24 hours.

Deleting your data

Two ways, both entirely under your control:

  • In the app: Settings → Reset the app. This clears all preferences, history, custom mitzvot, and scheduled reminders.
  • Uninstall the app. Since no copy exists on any server, uninstalling deletes everything.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

Any update is published at this address with a new effective date. A material change in how data is handled ships together with a new version of the app.
